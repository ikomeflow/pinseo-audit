Privacy, scope, and free-service limits

No login, analytics, or audit database is used. The app fetches the submitted public URL and validates its hostname through Cloudflare DNS. Website and hosting providers may log requests. Optional PageSpeed sends the URL to Google. Don’t submit private or sensitive links. Automated checks do not execute JavaScript or crawl the whole site. Hosting and Google API quotas may temporarily limit availability. No unavailable result is replaced with an invented score.
